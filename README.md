# Dr. Eng. Lamice Denguir – Professional Portfolio

"Bridging engineering research and software innovation to build practical solutions for industry and society."

Welcome to my professional GitHub portfolio.

I am a Mechanical Engineer (PhD), researcher, software developer, and entrepreneur with experience spanning academic research, industrial R&D, scientific communication, and software engineering. Throughout my career, I have designed and led multidisciplinary projects at the intersection of manufacturing, computational modeling, artificial intelligence, and digital product development.

This repository brings together the most significant outcomes of my professional journey, from scientific research and industrial engineering projects to modern software applications and technical documentation. It serves as a central hub where visitors can explore my work, understand my engineering approach, and access detailed documentation for each project.

## Repository Structure

The repository is organized into five main sections:

### 📐 Engineering Portfolio

A collection of the major engineering and research projects I have carried out throughout my career, organized by company, research laboratory, or institution. Each project presents its objectives, methodology, challenges, outcomes, and the engineering skills involved.

### 💻 Software Portfolio

A showcase of the software applications I have designed and developed, including project overviews, architecture, technical documentation, demonstrations, and development roadmaps.

### 📚 Scientific Portfolio

A collection of my scientific contributions, including peer-reviewed publications, dissertations, conference presentations, posters, and publicly available research reports produced by students I have supervised or mentored.

### 📝 Technical Notes

Engineering tutorials, technical notes, and educational resources covering topics related to engineering, software development, and digital technologies.

### 📰 Recognition & Outreach

Selected media coverage, interviews, articles, conference highlights, and social media communications featuring my professional activities and projects.

## Purpose

The objective of this repository is not only to archive my work, but also to document the engineering process behind each project, share technical knowledge, and demonstrate how research, software, and innovation can be combined to solve real-world engineering challenges.
