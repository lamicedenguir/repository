# Technical Notes

This section contains a selection of technical reference documents, engineering tutorials, and educational materials prepared by Dr. Eng. Lamice Denguir throughout her academic, industrial, and research activities. These documents were developed to document engineering methodologies, support research projects, facilitate knowledge transfer, and provide training for collaborators and students.

The collection is organized into the following categories:

## Courses

This folder contains the course material **"Non-Traditional Machining Processes"**, divided into three parts.

The first two parts are also available as video lectures on YouTube:

* **Part 1:** https://youtu.be/4UiY-csUxj8
* **Part 2:** https://youtu.be/kXlyXt-4X6k

## Tutorials

The tutorials are organized into three main subject areas.

### Material Modelling

This section contains two practical guides for identifying constitutive material model parameters:

* **EPNL_parameters_identification_procedure.pdf** — Elasto-Plastic Non-Linear material model parameter identification.
* **EVP_parameters_identification_procedure.pdf** — Elasto-Visco-Plastic material model parameter identification.

### Metal Cutting

This category is divided into two complementary sections.

#### Experimental Process

Engineering procedures covering:

* OFHC copper planing experiments
* Chip compression ratio measurement
* Milling force calculation using DasyLab
* Tool flank wear experimental protocol

#### Simulation

Simulation tutorials covering:

* ALE modelling applied to orthogonal cutting of OFHC copper
* Metal cutting simulation using Abaqus

### Surface Integrity

This section contains:

* A practical tutorial on metallographic specimen preparation for microstructural analysis.
* A comprehensive technical document describing the multi-physical analysis of corrosion resistance.
