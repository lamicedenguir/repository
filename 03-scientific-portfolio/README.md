# Scientific Portfolio

This section presents the scientific and academic contributions of Dr. Eng. Lamice Denguir throughout her research career in mechanical engineering and manufacturing sciences.

The portfolio includes peer-reviewed journal publications, conference papers, dissertations, presentations, posters, and other scientific communications that document her work in machining processes, material behavior, surface integrity, numerical modeling, and manufacturing engineering. It also contains a selection of publicly available internship reports produced by students under her academic supervision and mentorship.

Together, these documents reflect both her research activities and her commitment to scientific dissemination, engineering education, and knowledge transfer.
