# AMS Internship – Industrial Engineering & CAD Design (2010)

This section presents my worker training internship completed at **Ateliers Mécaniques du Sahel (AMS), Tunisia**, in 2010.

The internship provided my first experience within an industrial engineering environment, where I observed the organization of a manufacturing company while contributing to the design activities of the Engineering Office. It offered valuable insight into the daily practice of engineering, including production organization, multidisciplinary collaboration, and the product development process.

The work carried out during the internship is organized into two complementary parts.

---

## Part I – AMS: Activities & Departments

This study presents an overview of the company's organization and manufacturing activities.

Topics covered include:

* Company organization and departmental structure
* Roles and responsibilities of the various engineering and production departments
* Collaboration between technical and administrative teams
* Engineering workflow within an industrial manufacturing environment
* Relationship between academic engineering education and industrial practice

**Engineering Skills Developed**

* Industrial organization
* Manufacturing workflow analysis
* Engineering project lifecycle
* Cross-functional collaboration
* Industrial engineering fundamentals

---

## Part II – Valve Design

This project presents the design work carried out within the Engineering Office on stainless-steel valve components.

Activities included:

* 3D modelling of mechanical components using **CATIA V5**
* 3D modelling using **SolidWorks**
* Preparation of 2D engineering drawings with **AutoCAD**
* Technical documentation supporting product development

**Engineering Skills Demonstrated**

* Mechanical design
* Computer-Aided Design (CAD)
* Engineering drawing
* Technical documentation
* Product development
* Design office practices

---

## Software Used

* CATIA V5
