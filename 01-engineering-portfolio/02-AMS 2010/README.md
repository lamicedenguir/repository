# AMS Internship – Industrial Manufacturing & Mechanical Design (2010)

This section presents the engineering internship completed at **Ateliers Mécaniques du Sahel (AMS), Tunisia**, in 2010.

The internship provided my first immersion into an industrial manufacturing environment, allowing me to bridge academic engineering principles with real-world production processes. Beyond observing manufacturing operations, I participated in technical activities related to machining, production methods, engineering standards, and mechanical design documentation.
