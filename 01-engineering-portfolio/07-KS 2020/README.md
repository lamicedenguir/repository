# Knowledge & Skills (2020–2022)

### Engineering Education & Software Startup – Founder & CEO

This section documents the activities of **Knowledge & Skills SAS**, a software startup founded and led by Dr. Eng. Lamice Denguir between 2020 and 2022 in France.

The company focused on developing digital platforms for engineering education, training, and project management, bridging the gap between academic knowledge, industrial practice, and digital learning tools.

The company was formally closed in 2023 following its initial operational phase, after contributing to the development and validation of its core platforms.

---

## Company Mission

The objective of Knowledge & Skills was to design and deploy digital tools supporting:

* engineering training and education
* project-based learning environments
* academic and industrial collaboration
* structured knowledge transfer through software platforms

---

## Product Ecosystem

The startup developed and operated three main software platforms:

### KS-Portal

A digital platform for engineering education and training management.

Key functionalities included:

* learning and training workflows
* project tracking and organization
* structured engineering knowledge delivery

This platform was used in funding applications and innovation programs and received recognition from multiple innovation initiatives.

---

### KS-Conference

A web-based system designed for scientific event management.

Key features included:

* conference organization workflows
* participant management
* program structuring
* communication tools for academic events

---

### KS-Journal

A digital journaling and documentation platform designed for structured knowledge recording and reflection in engineering contexts.
