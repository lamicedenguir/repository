# ENIM Student Engineering Projects (2010–2012)

This section presents four multidisciplinary engineering projects completed during my studies at the **National School of Engineering of Monastir (ENIM), Tunisia**. Although developed as academic projects, each follows a professional engineering methodology and includes complete technical documentation prepared according to French (NF) and international (ISO) engineering standards.

Together, these projects demonstrate the progressive development of my skills in mechanical design, structural analysis, finite element simulation, automation, CAD, technical documentation, and systems engineering.

---

## Project 1 – Mechanical Gearbox Design (2010)

**Objective**

Design and fully document a speed reducer that satisfies predefined mechanical specifications.

**Engineering Skills Demonstrated**

* Mechanical system design
* Machine element sizing
* Mechanics of materials
* Strength and stress calculations
* Selection of standardized mechanical components
* Technical drawing
* Computer-Aided Design (CAD)
* Engineering documentation according to ISO/NF standards

---
